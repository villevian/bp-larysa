Meeting 1

4Client meeting: plan the evening event. You work for BCN Smart Events. Your teacher is the client. Meet (on a video call), ask questions, compare the venues and agree on the plan. Fill in your meeting notes.

🧑‍💼 Card A — you (event manager, BCN Smart Events)
  • Your client is a company with a two-day conference in Barcelona, 14–15 May.
  • Find out: how many guests? budget? special food? online guests?
  • Present the three venues. Compare them: cheaper than, the biggest, the best option for …
  • Your company wants a good result — but also a happy client. Try to sell the winery: you have a good discount there (10%).
  • At the end, check the plan and agree on the next step.

Meeting 2

5A problem! It’s two weeks before the event. You call the venue manager (your teacher). Explain the problem and agree on a solution.

📞 Card A — you (event manager)
  • The client now has 170 guests, not 140.
  • You booked the rooftop (max 120) — or the venue from Meeting 1.
  • Ask what the venue can do. Suggest solutions: How about …? Why don’t we …?
  • You can’t pay more than €1,000 extra.
  • Agree on a solution and on a date to confirm.

Useful: We have a problem with … · We need space for … more guests. · Is it possible to …? · What’s the best you can do? · OK, let’s agree on …

Meeting 3

6Bleisure. The conference finishes on Friday. You want to stay for the weekend in Barcelona. Ask your manager (your teacher).

🌴 Card A — you
  • Explain why the weekend is a good idea: the hotel is cheaper on Sunday, the flight is cheaper on Sunday too.
  • You can visit a new venue for next year’s event on Saturday morning (work!).
  • You pay for your hotel on Saturday night yourself.
  • Use comparatives: The Sunday flight is cheaper than the Friday flight.

Useful: Is it OK if I …? · It’s cheaper / better for the company because … · I promise I’ll … · Thank you, that’s great!

After the meeting

7Write a short follow-up email to the client (Meeting 1). Confirm what you agreed (60–100 words). Use your meeting notes.

  • Thank you for the meeting today.
  • Let me confirm what we agreed: …
  • Next step: …
  • Best regards,
