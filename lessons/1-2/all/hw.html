Reading

3A You are going to a conference in Barcelona (Thursday–Friday) and you want to stay for the weekend. Read about three hotels and answer the questions.

A Hotel Diagonal Business 8.9

💶 €190 a night · 🚶 to the conference centre: 5 minutes on foot

👍 meeting rooms, fast Wi-Fi, gym

👎 far from the beach (35 min)

B Casa Gòtic Boutique 9.3

💶 €140 a night · 🚶 to the conference centre: 25 minutes by metro

👍 rooftop bar, old town, quiet rooms

👎 small rooms, no gym

C Barceloneta Beach Hostel 7.8

💶 €85 a night · 🚶 to the conference centre: 40 minutes by metro

👍 next to the beach, free bikes

👎 noisy at night, shared kitchen

1. Which hotel is the cheapest?
2. Which hotel is the closest to the conference centre?
3. Which hotel has the best rating?
4. Which hotel is the best for a weekend at the beach?
5. Which hotel is better for working: A or B?
6. Which hotel is the most expensive?

B Which hotel would you choose for the conference and the weekend? Why?
