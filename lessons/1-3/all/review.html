Remember · Впізнай форму
Present Simple or Present Continuous?
🔁 Present Simple — usually, always, every day, never
habits, routines, facts, schedules
I work · She works · She doesn’t work · Do you work?
▶️ Present Continuous — now, at the moment, today, this week
actions in progress, temporary situations
I’m working · She’s working · She isn’t working · Are you working?

🇺🇦 Не вживаємо ‑ing з дієсловами стану: know, like, want, need, agree, understand → I agree, не I am agree.
