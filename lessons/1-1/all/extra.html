2Collocations. Choose the verb that goes with the noun.
  1. 1 a reservation
    We say make a reservation (= book). Do is for tasks/activities: do the shopping.
    Кажемо make a reservation. Do — для дій/справ: do the shopping.
  2. 2 a flight (= get on it in time)
    catch a flight = get to it in time. The opposite is miss a flight.
    catch a flight = встигнути на рейс. Протилежне — miss a flight.
  3. 3 a car for a week
    hire / rent a car = pay to use it. Borrow is free, from a friend.
    hire / rent a car — орендувати за гроші. Borrow — позичити безкоштовно.
  4. 4 at the hotel at 3 pm
    check in = register when you arrive; check out = leave and pay.
    check in — заселитися; check out — виселитися й розрахуватися.
  5. 5 an expense report
    submit an expense report = officially give it to the company.
    submit an expense report — подати звіт про витрати.
  6. 6 the plane
    board the plane = get on the plane. Boarding pass = the document for this.
    board the plane — сісти в літак. Звідси boarding pass.
Grammar & accuracy
3Prepositions. Choose the correct option.
  1. 1My flight was delayed two hours.
    Use by for the size of a change: delayed by two hours, prices went up by 5%.
    by показує, на скільки щось змінилося: delayed by two hours.
  2. 2I’ll meet you the gate.
    at for a point/place where you meet: at the gate, at the reception.
    at — для точки/місця зустрічі: at the gate.
  3. 3I go twice a year for work.
    abroad is an adverb — no preposition: go abroad, live abroad.
    abroad — прислівник, без прийменника: go abroad.
  4. 4We stayed a business hotel.
    stay at a hotel (also in a hotel). Never on.
    stay at a hotel (можна in). Ніколи не on.
  5. 5We arrived Berlin late at night.
    arrive in a city/country, arrive at a building/airport. Never arrive to.
    arrive in місто/країна, arrive at будівля/аеропорт. Не arrive to.
  6. 6It’s quicker to go train.
    by + transport without article: by train, by plane, by taxi. But on foot.
    by + транспорт без артикля: by train. Але on foot.
4Find and fix the mistake. Write the correct sentence or the correct part.
  1. 1I did a reservation for two nights.
    make, not do, with reservation.
    З reservation — make, а не do.
  2. 2The flight was late on two hours.
    Use by or the structure two hours late.
    Потрібно by або two hours late.
  3. 3Public transportations is cheap here.
    Transportation is uncountable — no -s.
    Transportation незлічуване — без -s.
  4. 4We arrived to the airport at 6.
    arrive at (airport, office), arrive in (city).
    arrive at (аеропорт), arrive in (місто).
  5. 5I travel to abroad a lot.
    No to before abroad.
    Перед abroad немає to.
  6. 6The flight was very bored.
    -ing describes the thing (boring flight); -ed describes how you feel (I was bored).
    -ing — про річ (boring flight), -ed — про почуття людини (I was bored).

Grammar: Present Perfect Continuous
★How it works
Form: have / has + been + verb-ing
I / you / we / theyhave been working= ’ve been working
he / she / ithas been working= ’s been working
QuestionHow long have you been working here?
Use: an action started in the past and is still going on now (or just stopped and you see the result).
for + a period (for two hours, for five years) · since + a start point (since Monday, since 2020)
🇺🇦 Дія почалася в минулому і триває досі. Якщо дія вже закінчилась (yesterday, last month, ago) — це Past Simple.
🇺🇦 Можна писати і повністю (I have been), і скорочено (I’ve been) — обидва варіанти правильні.
